# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

## The short version

Gustaro does not collect your personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. The App works on your phone and does not send what you do in it to us or anyone else.

## Who we are

Gustaro is provided by Paul Efrim and Petru Pripa. For any privacy question, write to paul.efrim@xtinct.ai or petru.pripa@yahoo.com.

## What stays on your phone

The App stores the following on your device only:

- Your recent scans, and your answer if you say whether you liked a wine
- Bottles you add yourself, including everything you type about them
- Your preferences, and the fact that you confirmed your age and accepted the terms

We have no access to any of this.

## Camera and photos

The camera is used to read barcodes and wine labels, and, if you choose, to take a picture of a bottle you add to the App. Reading happens on your phone using Apple's built-in technology. Images are not uploaded. A label photo is deleted right after its text is read. If you choose a photo from your library to read a label, only that photo is read, and the App keeps no copy of it. A picture you attach to a bottle you add is saved on your phone only, without its location or other hidden details, and is deleted with that bottle or with More > Delete my data.

## What we don't do

- We don't collect names, emails, locations or device identifiers
- We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services
- We don't show ads or track you across apps or websites
- We don't sell or share data

## Your choices

You can delete everything the App stores with More > Delete my data, or by deleting the App. You can turn camera access off at any time in your phone's Settings.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to reply, and delete it when it's no longer needed. You have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country. In Moldova that is the National Center for Personal Data Protection.

## Age

Gustaro is for adults of legal drinking age. It is not meant for children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the App.
