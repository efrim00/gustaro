# Expo demo script

Phone: iPhone 17 Pro Max. About 3 minutes. Works with no signal.

## Before the doors open

- Install the latest build and open it once.
- Put the demo bottles on the table: 2–3 in the catalogue, 1 that isn't.
- Check each one scans. Note which bottle needs a good light.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb. Set auto-lock to Never. Charge the phone.
- To reset between visitors: More → Delete my data.

## The demo

1. **Home.** "Gustaro tells you what's in a Moldovan bottle, in seconds, with
   information straight from the winemaker. No reviews, no scores."
2. **Scan a catalogue bottle.** Red camera button, point at the barcode. The wine
   page opens with the specs, sensory notes and pairings.
3. **Point out "Verified by the winemaker".** That's the promise.
4. **Read a label.** Scan the bottle that isn't in the catalogue by barcode, then
   tap "Read the label": "Not every bottle has a usable barcode."
5. **Add a bottle.** More → Add a wine bottle. Walk through the sections: photo
   (take one of the bottle), barcode, the wine, technical specifications, tasting. Save, then scan it: "It's
   recognised straight away, and marked as added by you, not verified."

## If something goes wrong

- Barcode won't read: more light, hold it flat and further away, or use "Type the code".
- Label not found: fill the frame with the front label, without glare.
- Camera refused: Settings → Gustaro → Camera.
- Someone asks about Romanian or Russian: "English first, the others are on the way."

## Worth knowing

- Bottles you add stay on that phone; they don't reach other people's apps.
- Wine descriptions show in Romanian until English versions are entered.
